This Privacy Policy explains how OT-Shield ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the OT-Shield mobile app and related services (the "Service"). We built OT-Shield to help Singapore workers record, understand, and protect their overtime pay, and we try to collect no more personal data than the Service actually needs to do that.

This policy is written to be consistent with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

1. Who We Are

OT-Shield is operated by Jeffrey Tong, an individual developer based in Singapore.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information, all of which you provide directly by using the Service:

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and sign in to your account via our authentication provider (Supabase Auth).
  • Overtime records: the details you record about your overtime — date, clock-in/clock-out times, calculated overtime duration and pay, hourly rate, and any shortfall between what you were paid and what our calculator estimates you should have been paid. We also store the raw text of what you typed or said when creating a record.
  • Employment/pay profile information: basic pay, hourly rate, shift type, normal working days, lunch break duration, and your normal daily start/end time — used to calculate your true overtime hours and pay more accurately.
  • Chat message content: the messages you type (or dictate, where voice input is supported) into the app. This content is sent to our workflow provider (Dify) and, through it, to OpenAI's API (currently GPT-4.1-mini) to understand your request and generate a reply.

We do not currently collect device identifiers, precise location, or behavioral analytics/usage-tracking data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and secure your account, and let you sign back in.
  • Record, calculate, and let you query, correct, or delete your overtime entries.
  • Estimate your true overtime pay and flag possible shortfalls.
  • Understand your messages and generate a relevant, correctly localized reply.
  • Diagnose and fix problems with the Service.

We do not use your data to show you ads, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Where and How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database, hosted in the Southeast Asia (Singapore) region (ap-southeast-1). Because this database is hosted within Singapore, your overtime and profile data does not leave Singapore as part of this storage arrangement.

Security measures currently in place: Row Level Security (RLS) policies restricting which rows a user can read or write; login sessions stored on-device via iOS Keychain (encrypted at rest); all network requests use HTTPS.

5. Third Parties We Share Data With

  • Supabase — database hosting and user authentication.
  • Dify, and through it, OpenAI (currently GPT-4.1-mini, accessed via OpenAI's official API — not Azure OpenAI, not a Dify-hosted proxy) — processes the text of your messages to understand your request and generate a reply. Per OpenAI's published API data usage policy, this data is not used to train their models by default, and is retained for at most 30 days for abuse-monitoring purposes before deletion.

We may also disclose information if required to by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of OT-Shield, our users, or others.

6. Your Rights

Consistent with PDPA, you have the right to access, correct, withdraw consent to, and request deletion of your personal data. Self-serve account deletion is not yet built into the app; until it is, contact us at jeffreytong95@gmail.com and we will act on your request within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After you request deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retaining certain records longer is required to comply with a legal obligation.

8. Children's Privacy

OT-Shield is intended for working adults. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect data from, children under 18.

9. International Data Transfers

Your Supabase database is hosted in Singapore, so no international transfer occurs for data stored there. Your chat message content is sent to OpenAI (via its official API) to generate a reply, which does involve processing outside Singapore.

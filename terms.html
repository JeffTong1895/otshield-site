1. What OT-Shield is

OT-Shield is an app that helps you record your overtime and estimate the overtime pay you may be owed under Singapore's Employment Act. It is run by TONG JUN HUI, an individual developer and Malaysian citizen.

The core of OT-Shield is free and will stay free: recording overtime, viewing and summarising your records, overtime-pay estimates, and basic information about your rights. Some optional extras may cost money — see section 7.

2. Not legal advice

OT-Shield explains, calculates and reminds. It does not decide whether your employer has broken the law, and it does not replace a lawyer, a union or the Ministry of Manpower (MOM). Pay figures are estimates based on public MOM rules and the details you enter, and they can be wrong. Before you act on a figure, check it — for example with MOM, the Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM) or your union.

3. Your records

Your records are your own personal notes. They are not an official company attendance system, and we do not claim they carry legal weight. Please enter information that is true to the best of your knowledge.

4. AI replies
